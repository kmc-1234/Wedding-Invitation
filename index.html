TELUGU VIVAHA VEDUKA · ఆచారాలు & సంప్రదాయాలు

Sacred Wedding Traditions

Every sacred ritual in our Telugu Vivaham carries profound timeless meaning, uniting two families and two souls for seven lifetimes.

01
Mangala Snanam & Pasupu (మంగళ స్నానం)

Purification with pure turmeric, fragrant holy waters, and sandalwood for divine grace and golden radiance.

02
Gauri Pooja & Kanyadaanam (కన్యాదానం)

Invoking Goddess Gauri, followed by parents lovingly placing the bride’s hand into the groom’s hand as Lord Narayana.

03
Jeelakarra Bellam (జీలకర్ర బెల్లం)

At the exact Muhurtham, cumin and jaggery paste are placed on each other’s heads, symbolizing an eternal, inseparable union.

04
Mangalyadharana & Saptapadi (మాంగళ్యధారణ)

Tying the sacred golden Mangalasutrams with three eternal knots, followed by seven sacred steps around the holy fire.
